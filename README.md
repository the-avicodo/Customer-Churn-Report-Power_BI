# Customer Churn Report - Power BI
### An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing customer churn and its main drivers to identify where revenue is leaking and why.
## What is churn and why does it matter?
Customer churn is the rate at which existing customers leave a company. In business, acquiring a new customer is typically harder and more expensive than keeping an existing one, so reducing churn is a priority.

## Business questions
In this project, I focused on three main questions:
1. What is the overall churn rate?
2. Which reasons cause the most customer churn?
3. What customer segments are most at risk of customer churn?
